One system, five stages

Every guest moves through the same five steps. When we switch the system on, we run each one for your studio.

  1. Found

    Your studio gets Google posts, review asks and local content, so it shows up first nearby.

  2. Clicked

    Every ad goes straight to the booking funnel with your offer on top.

  3. Booked

    The moment a lead clicks, the AI takes over. It texts first, calls if nobody answers, and books the slot itself. By text, from Mia on the phone, or in an Instagram DM.

    1. Within a minute

      It's Jess at Uni K Wax! What were you thinking about getting waxed?

      The AI replies first, every time, day or night. At Uni K Wax it answered 272 of 272 guest turns, median 14.5 seconds, and 38% of leads came in after hours.

    2. 10 minutes, no reply

      Mia calls. She offers two real openings at the nearest studio and books the one the guest picks.

    3. Booked

      On the calendar. Confirmation text, reminder texts, and the studio sees it in its booking system. If the guest texts back at any point, the text AI picks it up.

    Reply speed and after-hours share: Uni K Wax, Sep 15 to Oct 28, 2026.

  4. Kept

    Guests get a skin check at 24 to 48 hours, a “due for your next wax” text at 4 to 6 weeks, and a no‑show recovery text.

  5. Counted

    You get one scoreboard per studio, every week, with the dates each number covers.

We set your studio up as one row of settings. Prices, hours and the offer are typed once, and every channel reads them.
